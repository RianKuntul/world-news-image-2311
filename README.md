[![Reward](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261123/RianKuntul/world-news-image-2311/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261123)

Entry for make.fun's [news-image-20261123](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261123)
bounty, "Sum up 23 November's world news in one image".

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) publishes the committed
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` with an atomic write and a sha256
round-trip check. The image is a one-page front page of the world news
of Monday 23 November 2026, drawn with PIL and compiled from wire
reports published that day.

## The six stories

1. **Iran vows to use all tools against US naval blockade** — Tehran's
   foreign ministry said Monday it will confront the US naval blockade
   with every tool available, as tanker traffic through Hormuz stays
   slowed. (Reuters)
2. **Russian strike on Kyiv region kills 11** — Rescuers pulled the
   eleventh body from a flattened apartment block in Dymer after
   overnight missile and drone attacks across the region. (Reuters)
3. **Prabowo submits two VP candidates to parliament** — President
   Prabowo Subianto sends two names for the vacant vice presidency to
   lawmakers on Monday; parliament picks the winner on December 11.
   (Reuters)
4. **California sues OpenAI over model security** — Attorney General
   Rob Bonta filed suit against the AI company, citing model security
   and public safety risks in its systems. (Reuters)
5. **Delta automation glitch delays 1,000+ flights** — A system failure
   snarled departures across the US in Thanksgiving week, days after a
   separate fault forced a nationwide ground stop. (AP)
6. **France slides toward recession, PMI shows** — Private sector
   output fell to a six-month low in November as factory production
   posted its steepest drop since February 2025. (Reuters)
